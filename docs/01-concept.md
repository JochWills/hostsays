# 01 — Concept

## One line
HostSays is where travellers find and book experiences that local hosts actually recommend.

**Tagline:** Things to do, recommended by local hosts. Book in minutes.

## The problem
- Travellers don't know which local experiences are worth it. Global platforms list everything, with no local filter.
- Accommodation hosts recommend activities every day, for free, with no tracking.
- Small operators (game drives, seal snorkelling, sandboarding) rely on word of mouth and pay large commissions to global resellers.

## The solution
A marketplace with three sides:

| Side | Who | What they get |
|---|---|---|
| **Guests** | Tourists and local visitors | Trusted, host-vetted experiences; request-to-book; pay only a 10% deposit online |
| **Hosts** | Guesthouses, B&Bs, self-catering, Airbnb hosts, hotels | A free storefront page of their recommended experiences, and 6% commission on bookings their guests make |
| **Operators** | Game reserves, dive centres, charters, tours, adventure activities | Bookings from travellers, no upfront or monthly cost, 10% only on completed bookings |

## What makes HostSays different
- **Host recommendations are the product.** Every experience shows how many verified local hosts recommend it, plus their short tips.
- **Host storefronts** (`hostsays.com/[host]`) replace the paper "things to do" folder in the room.
- **Hosts are the distribution channel.** Guests arrive through a host's link or QR code, so we don't depend on Google traffic at launch.

## Launch
- **Region:** onboarding starts with Eastern Cape hosts and operators, then the rest of South Africa; other countries later.
- **Positioning:** the site must never read as limited to one area. Copy talks about South Africa as the current focus ("launching in South Africa first") and never about "the Eastern Cape coast". Towns only appear as examples or "popular places", never as the whole offer.
- **No fixed launch date and no minimum sign-ups.** Soft launch through hosts first; only show areas and categories that have live listings.
- **Aim:** be live before the December peak season if possible.

## Brand voice
Warm, local and honest, like advice from a good host over breakfast.
- Good: *"Ask any host in Summerstrand and they'll say the same thing: get on the water."*
- Avoid: hype, "unforgettable once-in-a-lifetime" clichés, and jargon.

## Trust promise (must appear on the site)
"Hosts earn a commission when you book through them." Recommendations must be genuine — see `02-business-rules.md`.
